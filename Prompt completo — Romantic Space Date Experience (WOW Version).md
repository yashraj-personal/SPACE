# Prompt completo — Romantic Space Date Experience (WOW Version)

Use this prompt in Claude Code / Cursor to build the project.

## Main Prompt

Build a cinematic, mobile-first interactive romantic website experience that feels like a beautiful digital surprise.

### Core idea
The story is:

**“She asked me to take her out… so I planned a trip across the solar system instead.”**

This is not a normal landing page.  
It should feel like:
- a romantic invitation
- a space journey
- a mini cinematic experience
- something emotionally impressive and visually unforgettable

The final result should make someone think:

**“Wow, he actually built this for her.”**

---

## Overall Creative Direction

The experience should combine:
- romance
- futuristic storytelling
- realistic space visuals
- modern premium UI
- immersive transitions
- a feeling of wonder

Tone:
- dreamy
- elegant
- intimate
- impressive
- playful but tasteful
- not childish
- not cheesy
- not tacky Valentine style

---

## Visual Style

Use a **super modern, ultra-polished, cinematic design**.

### Design style references
The aesthetic should feel like a mix of:
- luxury editorial design
- futuristic product UI
- cinematic space experience
- immersive storytelling website

### Visual mood
- deep black / midnight blue background
- glowing stars
- subtle nebula haze
- realistic planets
- premium typography
- elegant glass or translucent UI panels only if tasteful
- smooth gradients
- atmospheric lighting
- soft bloom and glow
- subtle film grain

### Important visual goal
The planets must feel like a **WOW moment**.

They should not just sit statically in the background.

They should:
- rise dramatically from the bottom of the screen
- slowly emerge into view
- feel huge and majestic
- fill a large part of the viewport
- have realistic texture, lighting and depth
- create a sense of scale and awe

Each major planet reveal should feel like a cinematic entrance.

For example:
- the user scrolls or clicks “next”
- space darkens slightly
- stars shift subtly
- then the planet rises up from below the screen
- it grows into view with parallax, glow and shadow
- it fills the scene in a striking, almost breathtaking way

The effect should feel modern and premium, like a next-generation immersive web experience.

---

## Planet Visual Requirements

The planets must look:
- realistic
- detailed
- high-quality
- modern
- spatially believable
- dramatic

Do not make them look like flat icons or childish illustrations.

They should feel inspired by real astronomical imagery.

### Planet reveal effect
For each stop, create a reveal where the planet:
- rises from the bottom edge of the screen
- slowly fills much of the viewport
- creates depth through scale and lighting
- has subtle motion
- is accompanied by UI text and route information
- feels like a giant celestial object entering the frame

### Especially important
Make **Saturn** one of the biggest hero moments.
Its rings should create an unforgettable scene.

Make **Moon** feel intimate and romantic.

Make **Jupiter** feel massive and majestic.

---

## Core Experience Structure

Create the experience as a guided story.

The user goes on a romantic trip across the solar system with another person.

There are two human figures:
- seen mostly from behind
- holding hands or sitting close together
- subtle silhouettes or simplified characters
- no need for close-up face realism
- elegant and emotional, not uncanny

The website should feel like the viewer is part of the date journey.

---

## Story Flow

### 1. Opening Scene
Start with a dark, cinematic screen with subtle stars and ambient motion.

Show this text sequence:

**“She asked me to take her out…”**

Pause.

Then reveal:

**“…so I planned something a little different.”**

Pause.

Then reveal:

**“Ready for our date?”**

Show a beautiful call-to-action button:

**“Start the trip”**

This first screen should already feel premium and mysterious.

---

### 2. Departure Scene
After clicking “Start the trip”, transition into outer space.

Show:
- Earth or low Earth orbit in the distance
- a small spaceship or viewing platform
- the two characters together, viewed from behind
- subtle stars and atmospheric glow

Text:
- **Departure: Earth**
- **Distance traveled: 0 km**
- or **0 miles** depending on config

Romantic caption:
**“Every good trip starts with a yes.”**

Show a route or travel timeline beginning.

---

### 3. Planet Journey Stops
Create a sequence of destinations.

Recommended stops:
1. Earth
2. Moon
3. Mars
4. Jupiter
5. Saturn
6. Neptune
7. Final destination

Each stop should include:
- planet name
- “Go to…” label
- distance data
- romantic caption
- massive realistic planet entrance
- visible travel progress
- elegant transition to the next stop

Possible final destination ideas:

Now I just have to make the real date live up to the space version.”**

## Motion Design Requirements

Motion is extremely important.

The animations should feel:
- smooth
- cinematic
- premium
- modern
- immersive

### Required motion ideas
- planets rising from below the screen
- subtle spaceship movement
- glowing route line animating between stops
- stars moving with parallax
- atmospheric light sweeps
- text fades and staggered reveals
- soft hover/tap interactions
- blur-to-focus transitions
- section-to-section cinematic transitions

### Planet entrance effect
Each time a planet appears:
1. UI fades slightly
2. stars shift softly
3. a glow appears near the lower edge
4. the planet rises upward from below
5. it scales into position
6. shadow, bloom and light create depth
7. text and distance data appear elegantly after the planet enters

This needs to be one of the signature visual effects.
